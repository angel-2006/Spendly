Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect the information you give us when you create an account, such as your name and email address, and the expense details you log, such as amounts, categories, dates and descriptions. We may also collect basic technical information, such as browser type, to keep the service running properly.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to provide and improve Spendly: to show your expenses, build your spending summaries, and keep your account secure. We do not sell your personal information or use your expense data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely and is kept only as long as your account is active. Passwords are stored in hashed form, not as plain text. You can delete your expenses or your account at any time, and we will remove your data from our systems.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly uses a small number of third party services, such as Google Fonts for typography. These providers may receive technical information like your IP address when your browser loads their content. We do not share your expense data with them.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact the Spendly team through the contact details provided on our website.
